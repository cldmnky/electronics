# JLCPCB order guide (fabrication + assembly)

Production artifacts are generated by `bun run release` in
`froemix/tscircuit/`; never hand-edit the files in `froemix/fab/`. Prototype
checks are not production approval. The physical acceptance gate must pass
before production exports; unmeasured first-article items remain pending.

## Files to upload

| Step on jlcpcb.com | File |
| --- | --- |

## Board options

- **4 layers**, 1.6 mm, 1 oz outer copper. **Bottom copper carries a solid GND
  plane** (see the GND plane note below); the inner layers are signal/return,
  not planes.
- **Dimensions** 80.9 × 128.5 mm (16 HP × 3U), 4 × M3 mounting holes 3.5 mm from
  the corners (3.2 mm drill).
- **Min trace/space** 0.25 mm (well inside JLCPCB's 0.09 mm 4-layer capability),
  **min via** 0.2 mm drill / 0.45 mm pad, **edge clearance** 0.4 mm.
- **Surface finish**: select **lead-free HASL** for the base board. Confirm it
  is accepted for this board and SMT assembly in JLCPCB's quote and inspect the
  assembly preview. The Daisy plugs into separately purchased hand-soldered
  female sockets; ENIG is not required merely to seat those sockets.

## Assembly: what JLC can and cannot place

`froemix-cpl-jlc.csv` already excludes the **10 through-hole parts**:

`CN1`, `CN2`, `CN3`, `CN4` (2.00 mm headers), `H1` (right-angle PMOD),
`J1` (2×3 MIDI header), `J2` (Eurorack 2×8 shrouded), `C24`, `C25`
(8 mm radial electrolytics), `U16` (B2405S SIP module).

They are in the combined reference BOM and hand BOM, but not the SMT upload BOM
or CPL. Source hand-fitted parts through **RS, Electrokit or DigiKey with EU
sales**, with exact identity and fit reviewed first. LCSC numbers are design
references, not approved hand-part purchases. Also source two 1×20 female
2.54 mm Daisy socket strips and two J1 shunts: these loose parts are not in
the generated BOM. The socket and shunt identities remain unqualified; see
[`ASSEMBLY.md`](ASSEMBLY.md) and [`MECHANICAL-STACK.md`](MECHANICAL-STACK.md).

This packet assumes **SMT only + separately qualified hand assembly**. A
JLCPCB through-hole assembly order is a different process and needs its own
validated BOM/CPL packet and sourcing review; do not manually add designators
back to this CPL.

**Single-sided SMT assembly:** all 107 SMT placements are on the **bottom**.
Select bottom-side-only assembly and verify the JLC preview. The mating headers
and J1 remain on top, but are through-hole parts fitted by hand after SMT; they
do not require a second SMT placement pass.

The three mating headers are hand-fitted THT: CN1/CN2 are C2883069, CN4 is a
C2883061 eight-way strip **prepared to seven ways before fitting**. Do not accept
an old short A2005WV substitute; the stack needs the 6mm shoulder. See
[`MECHANICAL-STACK.md`](MECHANICAL-STACK.md).

**Bottom-side rotation:** the exported CPL is in JLCPCB's convention (X/Y in mm,
bottom layers mirrored, rotation in degrees CCW). All SMT placements are 0°/180°:
101 of the 107 bottom placements are at 180° and 6 at 0°. H1 is a hand-soldered
through-hole part, not in the SMT CPL. **Check the 3D
preview of the assembled board on the order page before paying** — a wrong
bottom-side rotation is the classic failure here, and it is free to check.

## Parts already covered / excluded

- **Daisy Seed** is `doNotPlace` in the design (you own it). It is therefore
  absent from both the BOM and the CPL — intended. Solder its two female socket
  strips by hand and plug in the module after rail checks; do not solder the
  module directly to the board.
- **Fiducials and test points** are bare copper, `doNotPlace`, and absent from
  the BOM/CPL.
- Every assembled part carries an LCSC part number; `bun run check:parts`
  validates them against the live JLCPCB API (type, value, package).

## Fiducials

Four 1 mm bare-copper dots with a 0.5 mm mask window, no paste, at
(±33, ±63) mm — one per quadrant, inside the mounting-hole keepouts. JLCPCB's
assembly service only requires fiducials for large panels, but these make the
placement unambiguous and cost nothing. Verify in the order preview that the
dots appear as bright copper, not covered by mask.

## GND plane (bottom layer)

Five pour regions, 0.5 mm in from the edge with 73 clearance holes:
approximately 7383.1 mm² total (71.0 % of the 80.9 × 128.5 mm board),
0.3 mm clear to other copper.
GND is *also*
routed as explicit 0.25–0.3 mm traces because the router does not route into the
pour, so the plane is a shield and a return layer, not the sole return path.
It is in `B_Cu.gbr`: after uploading, check the gerber preview shows the plane
with clear rings around every pad and hole. A solid plane makes the hand-soldered
THT ground pins (CN1 pin 16, CN2 pin 16, CN3 pin 6, CN4 pin 1, J2, H1, the Daisy
ground pins) need a hotter iron — use thermal reliefs only if hand-soldering
without a preheat plate.

## What is *not* in the design yet (read before ordering)

- **10 THT parts** are hand-solder (above).
- **CV scaling** relies on firmware calibration (±1 % resistors) — see
  `../tools/sim/RESULTS.md`.
- **Mux inputs** (`M_ADC_0..7`) are unclamped: pot/attenuverter wipers from this
  board's own `P3V3_A`/`GND` only, 0–3.3 V in every power order — the hard
  contract is `doc/FRONTBOARD-INTERFACE.md`.

## Order-day checklist

1. Close the recorded physical acceptance items, then run `bun run release`.
   It must refuse production export while physical evidence is missing, stale
   or pending, or any prototype gate step fails.
2. Upload `froemix-gerbers.zip`; confirm 4 layers, 80.9 × 128.5 mm, 3.2 mm holes.
3. Upload the **SMT-only BOM** `froemix-bom-smt.csv` and
   `froemix-cpl-jlc.csv`; confirm **107** SMT placements, all on the bottom.
   The combined BOM is a reference, not the SMT upload.
4. Inspect the assembly preview: fiducials bright, no part off-board, bottom
   rotations sane, Daisy footprint empty.
5. Select single-sided **bottom** SMT assembly; order a separate stencil only
   if you need one. Hand-solder the through-hole parts and Daisy sockets later.


## Which BOM to upload

Upload `*-bom-smt.csv`. The combined `*-bom-jlc.csv` is reference only.
`*-bom-hand.csv` is a design-reference list, not an approved EU shopping list;
review exact RS/Electrokit/DigiKey parts and mechanical compatibility first.
