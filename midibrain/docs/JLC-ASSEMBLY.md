# JLCPCB order guide (fabrication + assembly)

Everything here is generated by `make release` (runs `tscircuit/scripts/release.sh`);
never hand-edit the files in `midibrain/fab/`. `fab/` is wiped on every release.

**Status: prototype outputs, not a fabrication release.** `make release` runs the
physical acceptance gate (`hardware-acceptance.json` + `docs/evidence/`) before it
deletes or writes anything in `fab/`; every required item is still pending, so the
release stops at step 1 today. See [acceptance.md](acceptance.md) for the open
physical gates (Z stack, first article, feedthrough fit, light-pipe length, panel
finish acceptance) and [fab-check.md](fab-check.md) for the geometry results.
Do not pay for fabrication or assembly until those gates are resolved, and
**recheck every LCSC number for stock, package and side at order time** (owner
rule: a catalog C-number is not proof of fit or availability).

## Board options (all three)

- **2 layers**, 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper. Front 128 × 112 mm, rear 101.6 × 112 mm
  (20HP), panel 132.08 × 128.5 mm (26HP × 3U).
- **Min trace** 0.15 mm, **min via** 0.20 mm drill / 0.30 mm pad,
  **edge rule** 0.2 mm. The via pair is at the published JLCPCB *via* floor
  (0.15/0.25 mm), not a PTH annular claim and not HDI; the project target is
  0.20 mm drill / 0.45 mm pad and `make release` enforces it
  (see [fab-check.md](fab-check.md)). The strengthened NPTH minimum is 0.50 mm
  (published); the boards' smallest NPTH is 0.90 mm.
- **Surface finish**: select **lead-free HASL** for front and rear (no fine-pitch
  parts; THT controls are hand-soldered after SMT). Confirm in the quote.
- **Panel is special**: bare FR-4, 1.6 mm, **no solder mask either side**,
  white silkscreen, no copper or components. Confirm JLCPCB accepts a no-mask
  both-sides order **before paying** — this is still an open item.

## Assembly: what JLC can and cannot place

### Front (UI) board — single-sided SMT (top)

`front-cpl-jlc.csv` holds **31 SMT placements, all on the top** (panel side):
MIDI opto/drivers, all passives, LEDs and nav, under and around the LCD and
controls. Select top-side-only assembly. The release script pins this and
fails if a future edit moves any SMT part off the top side.

The rear face carries only the `J5` THT body (it must mate rearward into the
front-rear ribbon) plus THT solder tails — no SMT, nothing for JLC to place
there.

Excluded THT (kept in the BOM): `J1`–`J4` (TRS jacks, C381127), `SW1`–`SW6`
(buttons, C84931), `U6` (encoder, C143790), `J5` (2×13 header, C48687629).
`J1`–`J4`, `SW1`–`SW6` and `J5` are hand-solderable. **U6 is not recommended for
hand soldering:** its row says "qualified THT process required" (selective/wave
or a validated manual process with recorded profile and inspection) until
acceptance item `encoder_solder_process` records the process. It stays out of the
SMT CPL either way.

### Rear (base) board — single-sided SMT

`rear-cpl-jlc.csv` holds **20 SMT placements, all on the bottom** (C13/C14 were
added by the electrical work; the previous count was 18). Select bottom-side-only
assembly.

Excluded THT (hand solder): `J_PICO_L`/`J_PICO_R` (Pico sockets, PPTC201LFBN-RC),
`J_PI` (2×20 PEC20DAAN), `J_FRONT` (2×13 PEC13DAAN). These four have **no LCSC
number in the design** — source generic 2.54 mm parts for the bench; they are
in the hand BOM with a blank LCSC column by design, not by omission.

### Bottom-side rotation

The exported CPL is in JLCPCB's convention (X/Y in mm, bottom layers mirrored,
rotation in degrees CCW). The front CPL is all top-side rotations and the
rear is bottom-side (mostly 180°). **Check the assembled-board 3D preview on the order
page before paying** — a wrong bottom-side rotation is the classic failure and
it is free to check.

## Bench list

`sourcing-bom.csv` merges both boards' hand BOMs with the curated
`tscircuit/scripts/sourcing-extras.csv`: Raspberry Pi 5, Pico (+headers),
Waveshare 3.5inch DSI LCD (H) with its DSI cable, candidate HAT/cooler/SSD,
all panel feedthrough cables, ribbons, PSU, MIDI/audio/optical cables, mounting
hardware (standoff/spacer lengths TBD at mockup), light pipes, knob (TBD) and
rail screws. M.2 HAT+, Active Cooler and SSD are **candidates** — unproven in
the enclosure. The 3D-printed bracket STL is **stale — do not print as-is**.
The knob, button caps and nav actuator are not selected; the USB-C feedthrough
is rejected for 5 A; the encoder row asks for a qualified THT process.

## Parts already covered / excluded

- Every SMT line item carries an LCSC part number; the release script **fails**
  if any SMT placement lacks one. THT parts keep their LCSC numbers where they
  have them (front) and blank where generic (rear headers/sockets).
- No `doNotPlace` parts exist in this design; everything in the BOM is meant
  to be bought.
- **Counts are verified, not assumed.** The release compares the generated BOM
  and CPL placement counts with `jlc-order.json` (front 43/31, rear 24/20) and
  fails with the exact delta when a design change moves them. Update
  `jlc-order.json` from the generated output only.
- The sourcing list parses every row strictly (10 columns); a shifted row would
  hide a warning such as the 3 A rating of CAB-15455, so it is rejected instead.
  CAB-15455 is marked **REJECTED for the 5 A path**; an exact 5 A rated
  feedthrough is still to be selected.
- Fiducials: none in this revision. JLCPCB only requires them for large panels;
  the 128 mm boards place unambiguously, but verify in the order preview.
