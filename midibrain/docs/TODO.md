# TODO — open work

The single list of work that is **not implemented or not verified**. It is not a
plan, a schedule or a promise. Each row names what exists in the tree today, what
is missing, and what closes it.

**Physical acceptance is authoritative elsewhere.** `hardware-acceptance.json`
holds the 31 required items, all `pending`; `make gate` prints their state and
`make release` refuses while any is open. **All 31 items pending.** What each
evidence file must show, and how the hardware fingerprint works, is stated once
in [acceptance.md](acceptance.md) and [evidence/README.md](evidence/README.md).
This page does not restate that list. A physical gap that is already an acceptance
item appears here only when it needs a decision or a design change first.

What was decided, including every superseded value, is in
[decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Owner decisions needed

Nothing in the tree can close these; each needs a decision before work starts.

| Item | What is open |
|---|---|
| ESD and EMC scope | No ESD array, no EMC pre-compliance and no standards test exists. Commercial-product compliance is not established and no CE/FCC/ESD claim may be made. Decide the requirement and test plan, or record that neither is claimed. |
| MIDI external mispatch (±12 V) | The electrical checks model the board rail only. A ±12 V source driven into a MIDI input or output (wrong cable, phantom power, miswired interface) is outside every model. Decide the survival requirement first: the test can destroy a board (`midi_current_faults`). |
| Encoder fit at the 10.6 mm stack | The `PEC11R-4015F-S0024` shows 0.9 mm of M7 thread past the panel front against a 2.5 mm nut + washer (1.6 mm short, 2.1 mm worst case), so it cannot be nutted. The remedy is an owner call: a longer-bushing part (`PEC11R-4020F-S0024` is a **lead only**: LCSC `C143788`, no main-shop SKU, 0.4 mm nominal spare and −0.1 mm worst case), a different encoder, or a stack change. |
| Knob bore vs shaft | The knob's D-bore cannot pass the round Ø6.0 part of the shaft: 1.2 mm of round shaft must enter a bore whose flat is 1.6 mm from the axis, i.e. 1.4 mm radial interference. Either the knob geometry changes or a shaft with the flat lower is chosen; nothing may be assembled as drawn. |
| Pi module x-position in the row | `J_PI` stays at x = 0 on the rear board, so the ribbon's lateral route and length are unset until the Pi module's position beside the main module is chosen. |
